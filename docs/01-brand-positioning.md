Primary: small and mid-sized online businesses (e-commerce, coaches, service businesses, SaaS) and marketing agencies in the US, UK, EU and Gulf.

## Positioning pillars

1. **AI Automation Engineer**: n8n/Make workflows that remove manual work.
2. **AI Agent Builder**: CrewAI/LLM agents, chatbots and assistants that do real tasks.
3. **AI Marketer**: AI content pipelines, lead gen, outreach and reporting.
4. **AI Vibe Coder**: ships websites, landing pages and internal tools fast.

Glue message: *one person who builds the site, the funnel and the automations behind it.* Most competitors do only one of these.

## Voice

- Plain, confident, practical. Talk in hours saved, leads captured, money made.
- No hype words ("revolutionary", "cutting-edge", "unleash"). No robot or brain imagery.
- Show the actual workflow (screens, node graphs, numbers), not abstractions.
- Short sentences. Second person ("you"). Honest about what AI can't do.

## Proof strategy

- Real case studies with numbers (see `05-case-studies.md`).
- Live demos: visitors can try an agent or calculator on the site.
- Workflow screenshots and Loom walkthroughs.
- Testimonials with name, role, company and photo where allowed.
- The site's own lead system is built with n8n and documented as a case study ("this form you just filled is automated").

## Differentiators

- Builder, not just a consultant: end-to-end from website to automation to agent.
- Transparent pricing ranges and fixed-scope packages.
- Fast delivery: first working workflow in days, not months.
- Handover: docs, Loom videos and ownership of every account and workflow.
