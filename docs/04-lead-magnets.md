# 04 · Lead Magnets & Funnel

Research takeaway: interactive tools (calculators, quizzes, audits) convert far better than PDFs. Reported quiz conversion is about 30–40%, against 3–5% for ebooks. Every magnet below gives real value first, then asks for an email to send a personalised result.

## Funnel

```
Traffic (LinkedIn, X, YouTube, Upwork profile, SEO, cold email)
   → Free tool / content
   → Email capture (result sent by email)
   → Nurture sequence (5 emails / 10 days)
   → Free audit or discovery call
   → Quick Win (paid) → Package → Care plan
```

## Phase 1 (launch with these)

### 1. Automation ROI Calculator (`/free/roi-calculator`)
- Inputs: team size, hours/week on repetitive tasks, hourly cost, tasks list (checkboxes: lead follow-up, data entry, reporting, support, invoicing).
- Output on screen: hours and money saved per year, payback period for a Quick Win.
- Gate: "Email me the full breakdown + 3 automations for my business" → PDF/email generated by n8n with an LLM.
- CTA after result: book a free audit.

### 2. Free Automation Audit (`/audit`)
- Highest intent. Form: website, biggest time-waster, tools used, email.
- Delivery: personalised 5–10 min Loom within 48 h showing 3 automation opportunities.
- Cap it ("5 audits per week") to keep quality and create scarcity.

### 3. n8n Template Pack (`/free/templates`)
- 5–10 ready-to-import workflows: lead → CRM → Slack alert; AI email triage; form → AI reply; content repurposer; invoice reminder.
- Gate: email → download link + setup video.
- Attracts DIY builders and agencies who later hire for complex work.

## Phase 2 (after launch)

### 4. AI Readiness Scorecard (`/free/scorecard`)
- 10 questions → score 0–100 across Lead handling, Operations, Marketing, Support, Data.
- Result page with tier ("Manual", "Getting there", "AI-ready") and top 3 recommendations.
- Segment tag sent to CRM for tailored nurture.

### 5. Email mini-course: "Automate Your Business in 7 Days"
- One small automation per day; each email links to a template and a 3-min video.

### 6. Prompt & AI Marketing Toolkit
- Notion pack: 50 marketing prompts, a content calendar, outreach scripts.

### 7. Live demo agent
- Chat widget on the site that answers questions about services and books calls. It is a lead magnet and a portfolio piece at once.

## Nurture sequence (after any opt-in)

1. Deliver the resource + a one-line intro.
2. Case study: one before/after story with numbers.
3. "The 3 automations every online business should have first."
4. Behind the scenes: a Loom of a workflow being built.
5. Offer: free audit (limited slots) → book link.
Then a weekly or bi-weekly newsletter ("AI Ops Notes").

## Capture system (dogfood it)

```
Next.js form → API route (zod validation, Turnstile, rate limit)
  → n8n webhook (secret header)
     → Google Sheets / Airtable / HubSpot (contact + tags + source)
     → LLM personalises result email → Resend / Brevo
     → Slack / WhatsApp alert to Ali
     → Add to nurture list
```

Track: visitor → tool start → completion → email → call booked → client, per magnet (see `09-seo-and-analytics.md`).

## Placement

- Home: calculator band and audit CTA.
- Every blog post: a relevant inline magnet (not a generic newsletter box).
- Exit intent (desktop only, once per visitor): template pack.
- `/free` hub page listing every tool.
