# 03 · Services & Pricing

Prices are **starting ranges in USD**, based on 2026 market research (setup fees of $1.5k–5k plus $500–2k/month retainers are typical for small automation agencies). Adjust them to your demand and confidence; start at the lower end while case studies build up.

## 1. Workflow Automation (`/services/automation`)

Remove repetitive work with n8n, Make or Zapier.

Examples: lead capture → CRM → instant follow-up; invoice and payment flows; Shopify order ops; report generation; Google Workspace / Notion / Airtable syncs; Slack and email alerts.

| Package | Scope | Price | Timeline |
|---|---|---|---|
| Quick Win | 1 workflow, up to 3 apps | $300–600 | 3–5 days |
| Ops Pack | 3–5 connected workflows + docs | $1,200–2,500 | 2–3 weeks |
| Full System | Process audit + end-to-end automation | $3,000+ | 4–6 weeks |

## 2. Custom AI Agents (`/services/ai-agents`)

LLM agents that answer, research, qualify and act.

Examples: website/support chatbot trained on your docs (RAG); lead-qualifying agent; inbox triage and draft replies; research agents with CrewAI; WhatsApp assistant; voice agent (Vapi).

| Package | Scope | Price |
|---|---|---|
| Starter Bot | FAQ/support bot on your content, 1 channel | $500–1,000 |
| Business Agent | Agent with tools (CRM, calendar, email) | $1,500–3,500 |
| Multi-Agent System | CrewAI crews, custom logic, dashboards | $4,000+ |

Running costs (LLM API and hosting) are billed to the client's own accounts.

AI-powered growth systems, built once and run every day.

Examples: content repurposing pipeline (1 video → posts, blogs, emails); AI lead gen and enrichment; personalised cold outreach; social scheduling; SEO content briefs; ad/KPI reporting dashboards.

## Offer mechanics

- **Free entry offer:** 15-min automation audit (Loom teardown) → paid Quick Win.
- **Payment:** 50% upfront, 50% on delivery; retainers monthly in advance (Wise, Payoneer, Stripe link).
- **Guarantee:** "If the workflow doesn't do what we scoped, I fix it free for 30 days."
- **Ownership:** client owns all accounts, workflows and code; full handover docs and Loom.
- **Agency white-label:** NDA, your branding, wholesale rate (about 20% off list).
