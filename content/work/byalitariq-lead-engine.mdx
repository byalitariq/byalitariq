## Snapshot

This site is my own first client. Every form on it, from the contact page to the free audit, runs through one lead system I built with Next.js and n8n. This page is a live build log: results will be added here once there's real data to show.

## Challenge

A freelancer's site usually sends form entries to an inbox and hopes someone replies in time. Replies take hours, there's no record of where a lead came from, and every new lead magnet needs its own form and its own logic.

## Solution

One endpoint handles every form:

1. **The form** validates input in the browser with the same schema the server uses.
2. **`POST /api/lead`** rate-limits requests, checks a Cloudflare Turnstile token, drops honeypot bots and validates again with zod.
3. **The n8n webhook** receives the clean lead with a secret header, so the URL is never exposed to the browser.
4. **The workflow** de-duplicates by email, stores the lead with its source and tags, sends a personal reply and alerts me.

Adding a new lead magnet means adding a new `source` value, not a new backend.

## Stack & how it runs

Next.js on Vercel, zod for validation, Cloudflare Turnstile for spam, n8n for the workflow and Resend for email. Running cost is a few dollars a month at this volume.
